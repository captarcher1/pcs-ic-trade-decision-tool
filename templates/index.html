More details & tips

Verdict colors

GO All hard filters pass. Trade it within your sized contracts.
CAUTION No hard fails, but one or more warnings (e.g. IVR 15–29%, VIX 20–25, credit below 10%). Review warnings before entering — reduce size or skip if multiple flags.
NO-GO A critical filter failed (e.g. VIX ≥ 30, IVR < 15%, Monday gap outside Friday range, DTE outside 21–35 days, expectancy negative). Do not trade.

Input definitions

VIX: market-wide implied volatility. <20 calm, 20–25 elevated, ≥25 caution, ≥30 hard stop.
ATR(5): 5-day average true range in points. Used with the multiplier and DTE to calculate safe strike distance.
IV Rank: where current IV sits in its 1-year range (0–100%). Higher IVR = richer premium to sell. ≥30% passes, 15–29% warns, <15% NO-GO.
Trend vs 20 EMA: above EMA uses 1.5× multiplier (bullish bias); below EMA uses 2.0× (more cushion needed).
Short-DTE boost: multiplier auto-increases by +0.5 at 1-week DTE and +0.3 at 2-week DTE to compensate for gamma risk.
Monday gap: if today's open is inside Friday's range, the trade is tradable. Outside = skip (regime shift signal).

Position sizing rule

Maximum risk per trade is capped at 5% of account size. The Contracts input will turn red if you exceed this. Reduce contracts or widen the wings.

Tax & settlement footnote

The bottom footnote changes by ticker — broad-based index options (SPX/XSP/NDX/RUT) get Section 1256 60/40 tax treatment; SPY/QQQ/IWM and single stocks get standard equity-option tax treatment with assignment risk.

Data source

The active data source is shown in the title badge. Change it in your .env file (DATA_SOURCE=yfinance|finnhub|alphavantage|polygon). Spot and ATR auto-fetch for supported tickers; index quotes (SPX, NDX, RUT) require manual entry on free tiers.
