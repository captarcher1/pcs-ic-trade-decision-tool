How to use this tool
1
Pick mode & ticker. Choose Iron Condor or Put Credit Spread. Enter the ticker (SPX, SPY, NDX, RUT, a stock…). Spot price and (for SPX / XSP / SPY) IV Rank fill in by themselves, and strike rounding and the tax footnote adjust.
2
Enter market inputs. Type VIX and ATR(5). Check IV Rank and spot (↻ refreshes spot); you can overwrite either one.
3
Set trade parameters. DTE in weeks, trend vs 20 EMA, Monday gap, account size, wing width and contracts. Credits come from step 4, so you don't need to type them.
4
Pick your strikes. The Strike candidates table lists real strikes from the option chain (short delta 15 or lower) for your ticker and DTE. For an iron condor, choose how to pair the short strikes, then click a row. For a put credit spread, click a put row. Strikes and credits fill in (mid), with bid / mid / ask shown under the credit boxes.
5
Check the verdict. Top box shows GO / CAUTION / NO-GO. Scroll down for each filter step, the strike map and per-contract expectancy. Choose which POP to use (hard-coded or delta-implied).
6
Review economic events. Right panel flags FOMC, CPI, NFP, PPI, GDP and PCE weeks. Avoid opening positions through high-impact releases.
More details & tips

Verdict colors

GO All checks pass. Trade it within your sized contracts.
CAUTION No hard fails, but one or more warnings (e.g. IVR 15–29%, VIX 20–25, credit below 10% of max risk, thin or negative expectancy). Review the warnings before entering — reduce size or skip if there are several.
NO-GO A critical check failed: VIX ≥ 25, IVR < 15%, Monday gap outside Friday's range, or DTE outside 2–6 weeks. Do not trade.

Input definitions

Ticker: the underlying. Spot fills in by itself (index quotes are delayed).
VIX: market-wide implied volatility. <20 calm, 20–25 elevated (caution), ≥25 NO-GO. Typed by you.
ATR(5): 5-day average true range in points. With the multiplier and DTE it gives a reference strike distance. Typed by you.
IV Rank: auto-filled for SPX / XSP / SPY from the 52-week VIX rank (a proxy, not a true per-ticker IV rank). Other tickers: enter it yourself. Editing the box makes it manual. ≥30% passes, 15–29% warns, <15% NO-GO.
DTE (weeks): the strike table uses the listed expiry closest to this many weeks out (within 5 days). 3–5 weeks is ideal; 2 or 6 warns; outside 2–6 is NO-GO.
Trend vs 20 EMA: above EMA uses a 1.5× ATR multiplier; below uses 2.0× (more cushion). Short-DTE boost: +0.5 at 1 week, +0.3 at 2 weeks. This gives the ATR reference strikes shown in the checks.
Monday gap: open inside Friday's range = tradable. Outside = skip.
Wing width: distance between the short and long strike. The strike table uses the put wing width for both sides of an iron condor, and selecting an IC row sets the call width to match.
Credits: filled in (at mid) when you click a row; you can still overwrite them with your actual fill. Bid / mid / ask of the selected row show under each credit box.
Contracts: position size. Max risk must stay within 5% of your account.

Strike candidates table

Shows up to 5 rows, closest to 15 / 13 / 11 / 9 / 7 short delta (never above 15). Each row shows delta, strikes, Bid / Mid / Ask, Net credit $ (mid × 100 × contracts), Max risk $ (PCS: (wing width − credit) × 100 × contracts; IC: the larger of the put and call side) and Credit / max risk (Net credit ÷ Max risk, green at 10% or more).
Iron condor pairing: Equal distance from spot puts both short strikes about the same number of points from spot (the call delta is lower because calls are cheaper than puts, so fewer rows may qualify). Equal delta tier pairs the same delta on both sides (more rows, but the call is usually closer to spot).
Strikes are hidden when the short leg has no bid, a wide bid-ask spread, no usable long leg at your wing width, or (single stocks) thin open interest. The source (Cboe, or yfinance as backup) and pull time show under the table. After a ticker, DTE or wing-width change the table reloads and your selection is cleared.
POP choice: in the expectancy card, pick the hard-coded POP (recommended: IC 72%, PCS 85%) or the delta-implied POP (calculated from the selected row). Expectancy is a warning only — it never causes a NO-GO.

Position sizing rule

Maximum risk per trade is capped at 5% of account size. The note under Contracts turns red if you exceed this. Reduce contracts or widen the wings.

Tax & settlement footnote

The bottom footnote changes by ticker — broad-based index options (SPX/XSP/NDX/RUT) get Section 1256 60/40 tax treatment; SPY/QQQ/IWM and single stocks get standard equity-option tax treatment with assignment risk.

Data source

The active data source is shown in the title badge. Change it in your .env file (DATA_SOURCE=yfinance|alphavantage|polygon; yfinance is the default). Spot auto-fetches for stocks, ETFs and the SPX / XSP / NDX / RUT indexes (delayed quotes). The Strike candidates table comes from the Cboe delayed option chain, with yfinance as the backup.
